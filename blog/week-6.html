Week 6: Ground Truth Nutrition: CIQUAL Integration and Auditable Database APIs

Welcome to Week 6 of building VITA! Over the last two weeks, we accomplished an engineering feat on our hardware bench: our Orange Pi 5 is running a fully containerized pipeline that captures speech, orchestrates intent with Qwen 2.5 1.5B, and speaks back in under 1.4 seconds. But as we tested it with real meals, we confronted a critical, dangerous flaw: the language model was making up nutritional numbers.

When asked for the calories in 150 grams of cooked lentils, Qwen would emit 170 kcal in one turn, 230 kcal in the next, and hallucinate arbitrary quantities of sodium and iron. For an autonomous device aspiring to be a medically defensive health guardian, statistical approximations are completely unacceptable.

This week, we solved the truth problem. We integrated the French government's authoritative national food composition database (ANSES CIQUAL), engineered our automated SQLite FTS5 importer, established our repository's data architecture, and enforced the most fundamental safety invariant in VITA: we strictly prohibit the LLM from writing SQL queries.

Why We Chose CIQUAL: The European Imperative

Most AI fitness and nutrition apps default to the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) FoodData Central database. But VITA's primary launch market is Europe. When you build in Europe, the nutritional reality is completely different:

  • European Dietary Specifics: Bread composition, dairy pasteurization, cured charcuterie, and regional culinary preparations in Europe differ markedly from American industrial food baselines.
  • Government Authority: ANSES CIQUAL (French Agency for Food, Environmental and Occupational Health & Safety) is compiled directly by government research laboratories. Every single nutrient record is backed by empirical wet-lab chromatography, spectrometry, and peer-reviewed citations.
  • Open Public Data Licensing: Unlike many European national datasets that are locked behind expensive commercial paywalls or restrictive academic licenses, ANSES publishes CIQUAL as free public open data, making it compliant with our 250€ one-time hardware purchase model.

The Importer & ETL Pipeline: Transforming Raw Government XML

Raw government datasets are notoriously messy. ANSES distributes CIQUAL as a series of massive XML files filled with French localized formatting. Our importer script in database_builders/ciqual_builder/importer.py performs extensive automated sanitization:

  • French Decimal Normalization: Converts French comma decimal strings (e.g. "24,6" → 24.6).
  • Qualitative Bound Resolution: Resolves laboratory uncertainty markers such as "< traces" (normalized to 0.0 with trace flag), "< 0,5" (set to the 0.5 upper bound for defensive caloric safety), and empty dashes "-".
  • Relational Table Normalization: Maps raw records into four clean tables: foods (keyed by alim_code), nutrients (keyed by const_code), food_groups, and the junction table composition containing teneur (nutrient quantity per 100 grams).
  • Full-Text Search (FTS5): Builds a virtual table foods_fts using the unicode61 tokenizer, enabling sub-millisecond keyword and fuzzy matching for both French and English food names.
database_builders/ciqual_builder/schema.sql SQL 
-- Authoritative food identity universe: ciqual.db owns alim_code
CREATE TABLE foods (
    alim_code    INTEGER PRIMARY KEY,
    nom_fr       TEXT NOT NULL,
    nom_eng      TEXT NOT NULL,
    grp_code     TEXT NOT NULL
);

-- Fast Sub-Millisecond Search Index on Embedded Flash
CREATE VIRTUAL TABLE foods_fts USING fts5 (
    alim_code UNINDEXED,
    nom_fr,
    nom_eng,
    content       = 'foods',
    content_rowid = 'alim_code',
    tokenize      = 'unicode61'
);

-- Laboratory-verified composition per 100g
CREATE TABLE composition (
    alim_code   INTEGER NOT NULL,
    const_code  INTEGER NOT NULL,
    teneur      REAL,
    confidence  TEXT,
    source_code INTEGER,
    PRIMARY KEY (alim_code, const_code)
);

The Core Safety Rule: Why We Never Let the LLM Write SQL

Many modern AI architectures take the lazy route: they expose a SQLite connection to the LLM and prompt it to "Write a SQL query to answer the user's question." In a consumer medical health appliance, this is a catastrophic design mistake.

Figure 2: Comparing uncontrolled LLM-generated SQL against VITA's defensive, auditable Knowledge API gateway.

We designed the system so that the LLM never sees a SQL prompt. Instead, we created an auditable Python API layer in core/knowledge/repositories/ciqual.py:

core/knowledge/repositories/ciqual.py Python 
# Safe, prepared parameterized search — immune to injection and hallucinations
class CiqualRepository(BaseReadOnlyRepository):
    def search_foods(self, query: str, limit: int = 5) -> list[FoodSummary]:
        normalized = query.strip()
        with self.connect() as connection:
            rows = connection.execute(
                """
                SELECT f.alim_code, f.nom_eng, f.nom_fr, f.grp_code, f.ssgrp_code
                FROM foods_fts ft 
                JOIN foods f ON f.alim_code = ft.alim_code
                WHERE foods_fts MATCH ? 
                ORDER BY rank LIMIT ?
                """,
                (f'"{normalized}"', limit),
            ).fetchall()
        return [FoodSummary(r[0], r[1], r[2], r[3], r[4]) for r in rows]

    def get_nutrients(self, alim_code: int) -> list[NutrientItem]:
        with self.connect() as connection:
            return connection.execute(
                """
                SELECT c.const_code, n.nom_eng, c.teneur, c.confidence, s.ref_citation
                FROM composition c
                JOIN nutrients n ON n.const_code = c.const_code
                LEFT JOIN sources s ON s.source_code = c.source_code
                WHERE c.alim_code = ?
                """,
                (alim_code,),
            ).fetchall()

Deterministic Mass Normalization Math

Every food item in CIQUAL is indexed strictly per 100 grams of edible portion. When a user reports eating 180 grams of grilled salmon, our KnowledgeService calculates the exact nutritional quantities using deterministic floating-point arithmetic rather than probabilistic LLM approximations:

The Proportional Mass Scaling Formula

For any nutrient i with reference laboratory concentration Ti (teneur per 100g) and user portion mass M (in grams):

Actual Amount = (Ti / 100.0) × M

For 150g of Atlantic Salmon (CIQUAL alim_code = 20005, where Protein T25000 = 24.6 g / 100g and Energy T328 = 216 kcal / 100g):

  • Protein: (24.6 / 100.0) × 150 = 36.90 grams
  • Energy: (216.0 / 100.0) × 150 = 324.00 kcal
  • Vitamin D: (8.7 / 100.0) × 150 = 13.05 μg

The Two-Stage Grounded Interaction Flow

With CIQUAL compiled and our Knowledge API active, our conversational interaction loop operates with mathematical defensiveness:

  1. Voice Capture & Transcription: The user says: "I just had 200 grams of cooked quinoa for lunch." Whisper emits the plain text transcript in 0.35s.
  2. Semantic Slot Extraction: Qwen 2.5 1.5B inspects the text and outputs structured JSON in 0.45s: {"intent": "LOG_FOOD", "food_query": "quinoa", "portion_grams": 200.0}.
  3. Database API Verification: The orchestrator passes "quinoa" to CiqualRepository.search_foods(), resolving to alim_code = 20591, and computes the exact 200g nutritional profile in 0.52 milliseconds.
  4. Template Injection & Playback: The verified numbers are injected into our deterministic voice template and spoken by Piper TTS: "Logged 200 grams of cooked quinoa. That provides 286 calories, 8.8 grams of protein, and 50.6 grams of carbs."

Week 6 Wrap-Up & Looking Ahead to Week 7

Week 6 marked the transition of VITA from a clever tech demo into a scientifically defensive nutrition platform:

  • Anchored our intelligence in ANSES CIQUAL, providing over 3,100 laboratory-verified food records tailored to the European market.
  • Engineered our SQLite FTS5 importer, achieving sub-millisecond keyword lookup times on our local hardware.
  • Enforced strict architectural separation: the LLM extracts semantic slots, while an auditable, parameterized Database API executes queries.
  • Completely eliminated nutritional hallucinations: every single calorie and macronutrient is backed by a verifiable government paper trail.

Yet whole food intelligence does not stop at calories and macronutrients. Food has an ecological footprint, complex culinary preparations, and critical allergen dangers that can trigger anaphylaxis.
